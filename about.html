About Juma Electricals

Home / About

Built on Expertise, Driven by Purpose

Juma Electricals & Engineering Firm was established in 2010 by Eng. Juma Hassan, a certified electrical engineer with a passion for delivering safe, innovative and affordable electrical solutions to communities across the region.

What started as a two-man operation has grown into a fully-fledged engineering firm with a team of over 30 certified professionals, serving residential, commercial, industrial and institutional clients with distinction.

  • Founded 2010 — over 14 years of proven expertise
  • Fully licensed by the Energy Regulatory Commission
  • Serving clients in Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu and beyond
  • Committed to green energy and sustainable solutions
